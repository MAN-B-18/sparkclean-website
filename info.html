About SparkClean

SparkClean began as a simple idea: make professional cleaning affordable and easy for busy people.

Founder: Kgotso Masuku

Kgotso started SparkClean while studying at university after seeing classmates struggle to balance work and house chores. What began as weekend jobs grew into a trusted cleaning service focused on reliability and sustainability.

Kgotso's goal is to create jobs, deliver consistent quality, and make cleaning services accessible for students and small businesses.

Our Values

  • Customer-first service
  • Eco-friendly cleaning
  • Transparent pricing
  • Safety and trust
